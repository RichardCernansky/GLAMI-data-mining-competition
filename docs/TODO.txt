- more engineered features:
    More engineered features — means creating new columns from the existing data that capture patterns the current features miss. Some ideas:
    Brand extraction — titles often contain the brand name ("Soma JACQUEMUS", "Τσάντα JACQUEMUS"). If you extract the brand string and check if two items share it, that's a very strong signal. You could do this with a simple heuristic: the last word or any word that appears in multiple geos.
    Title word overlap — count how many words two titles share after lowercasing. "Baleríny Clarks" and "Balerinke Clarks" share "Clarks" even without embeddings.
    Price rank within geo — instead of raw EUR price, compute whether two items are in the same price percentile within their respective geos. A €50 item in Italy and a €50 item in Greece are in similar market positions.
    Department + color combo — instead of separate Jaccard scores, check if two items have the exact same department AND color combination.
    Description length similarity — items that are the same product from different shops might have similar description lengths.
