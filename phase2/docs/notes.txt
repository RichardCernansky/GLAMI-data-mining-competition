Product groups with confidence

Divide the space into regions and only search nearby regions:

n_cells=400 — how many Voronoi regions to split the embedding space into
200k items → divided into 400 cells → ~500 items per cell on average

n_probe=20 — how many cells to search when answering a query
When you ask "find neighbors of item X", FAISS finds X's cell, then searches that cell plus the 19 nearest neighboring cells. It skips the other 380 cells entirely.
